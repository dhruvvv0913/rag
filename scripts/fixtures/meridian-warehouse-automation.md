# Meridian Logistics Warehouse Automation Pilot — Results Summary (March 2025)

## Overview

Meridian Logistics ran a twelve-week automation pilot at its Nashik distribution
center between November 2024 and January 2025, deploying 46 autonomous mobile robots
(AMRs) alongside its existing workforce of 310 warehouse staff. The pilot was led by
COO Priya Deshmukh and integrated the robots into the pick-and-pack workflow for
small-parcel orders only; palletized freight was excluded.

## Results

- Average order picking time fell from 34 minutes to 19 minutes for small parcels,
  a 44 percent reduction.
- Picking accuracy improved from 98.1 percent to 99.4 percent.
- Overtime hours across the pilot period dropped by 27 percent.
- Two of the 46 robots were out of service at any given time on average, an uptime
  of 95.7 percent, below the vendor's promised 98 percent.
- Staff turnover during the pilot was unchanged, and the post-pilot survey found 61
  percent of floor staff preferred working with the robots, while 18 percent said the
  robots made their shift more stressful.

## Costs and decision

The pilot cost 2.1 crore rupees including integration work. Payback was modelled at
26 months at pilot performance levels. In March 2025 the board approved expanding
automation to two additional distribution centers (Indore and Coimbatore) by mid-2026,
while keeping palletized freight manual pending a separate forklift-automation study.
